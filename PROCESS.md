# Process overview

## What I built

A study-spot finder for ANU: a list of the main places a student could study
on campus, with details like opening hours and seat counts, plus student
ratings.

## How I got here

During ideation, AI wasn't much help. I can't tell whether that's because AI
is genuinely worse at this than a person, because my approach was wrong (I
mostly just asked "any good ideas?"), or because I already have strong
personal preferences about ideas. Either way, the idea itself came from
thinking it through myself (just found that running an LLM behind my own eyes doesn't cost tokens.)

Once past ideation, AI had an obvious tendency to throw out ideas without much
discipline (still my subjective read). This time I raised the effort level
and had it research real precedents and principles on its own, to reason
about which needs were real and which were pseudo-needs, before consolidating
a plan. The difference was clear — that plan turned directly into
[`2062074`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-crit7-blendspitfire/commit/2062074)
(fixing the review-validation bug, filters, the map link, the README) and
[`b322ac5`](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-crit7-blendspitfire/commit/b322ac5)
(the ANU-brand restyle and building photos).
